# Supporting Clash Command

Thank you for using **Clash Command**!

Clash Command was created with a simple principle: **an offline-first, lightning-fast, and distraction-free assistant for Clash of Clans players**.

---

## 🌟 Our Philosophy

- **100% Free & Open Source**: No paywalls, no "pro" tiers, and no locked features.
- **Strictly Offline-First**: All village profiles, levels, timers, and logs live on your device in SQLite. No cloud account required.
- **Zero Ads & Zero Tracking**: No banners, interstitial ads, trackers, or third-party telemetry.
